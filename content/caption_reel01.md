Why is this page called metacognition?

Because the smartest skill isn't knowing more — it's knowing what you actually know.

In 1979, psychologist John Flavell gave this ability a name: thinking about your own thinking. Since then it has become one of the best-documented ideas in learning science — and today it is quietly powering modern AI too.

🤖 WHY AI NEEDS METACOGNITION
The best AI systems run the same loop you should:
→ Self-reflection: agents critique their own answers, then retry and improve (Reflexion, NeurIPS 2023)
→ Self-monitoring: sampling several reasoning paths and voting for the most reliable one (Self-Consistency, ICLR 2023)
→ Calibration: a model that knows WHEN it is likely wrong hallucinates far less (Xiong et al., ICLR 2024)
An AI that monitors its own thinking makes fewer confident mistakes. Exactly like a human.

✦ METACOGNITION OUTSIDE THE CLASSROOM
• Programmers: predicting where the bug lives BEFORE running the code
• Doctors: asking "what would disprove my diagnosis?" before committing
• Athletes: re-watching your own game footage = monitoring + evaluation
• Writers: reading your draft like a stranger = evaluation
• Conversations: noticing "I didn't actually understand that" — and asking again

Same loop every time: PLAN → MONITOR → EVALUATE.
