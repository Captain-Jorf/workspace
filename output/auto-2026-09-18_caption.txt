Ever let an AI code suggestion take over without thinking?

AI can speed coding, but it can also hide gaps in our understanding.

✦ WHAT'S GOING ON
→ You see a suggestion, hit Tab, and trust it.
→ That shortcut can skip the mental check you need.

✦ THE METACOGNITIVE GAP
→ Metacognition is watching your own thinking.
→ Missing the check creates a gap between confidence and knowledge.

✦ REAL‑WORLD EXAMPLE
→ Koder offers a one‑line fix instantly.
→ Pressing Enter gives code, but you lose the chance to reflect.

✦ THE QUICK TECHNIQUE
→ Pause, read the suggestion out loud.
→ Ask if it truly solves the problem and can you explain it.
→ Write a short comment summarizing before you accept.
