Why does everything take twice as long as you promised yourself?

You plan the week, and it looks doable. Even relaxed. Then Thursday arrives and half the list is untouched.

🧠 WHAT'S GOING ON
→ When we plan, we imagine the best-case version of the task.
→ We picture ourselves focused, healthy, and uninterrupted.
→ We forget the interruptions, because they are not part of the story we tell.

✦ TRY THIS
→ Try this: before you estimate, ask how long similar tasks took last time.
→ Use that number, not the hopeful one.
→ Then add the interruptions you already know will come.

📚 SOURCES
1. Kahneman & Tversky (1979), in Progress in Social Psychology
2. Buehler, Griffin & Peetz (2010), Advances in Experimental Social Psychology
