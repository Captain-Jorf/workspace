Ever catch yourself nodding while your brain’s already elsewhere?

Attention slips in stand‑ups, especially with AI tools nudging you to type.

✦ WHAT'S GOING ON
→ Your mind swaps listening for drafting a reply.
→ That gap creates missed info and wasted time.

✦ THE METACOGNITIVE FIX
→ Spot the urge, pause, count, then refocus.
→ Write a quick note of what you missed.
